FM-1 Bank Collection

Thematic 32-voice DX7 banks for the M-VAVE FM-1. Load four into slots A/B/C/D and every one of the 128 presets is something you actually want.

— banks 32 voices each DX7 SysEx free
No matches.

Install: open fm1-editor.com in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 over USB-C, Import DX7 bank, pick a destination bank (A–D), then hold SAVE on the FM-1.

Banks are standard Yamaha DX7 32-voice bulk dumps (4104 bytes). M-VAVE's checksum convention is (-sum(4096 voice bytes)) & 0x7F — header excluded.

Generated with fm1_banks.py from public DX7 SysEx collections. Patch authorship belongs to the original DX7 programmers. Source on GitHub.
